# Overview
If you wish to use Nightlight's brand assets in your project or commercially, this is the place!

## Download the Nightlight Branding Kit

[Download the Branding Kit (.zip)](./assets/Nightlight_Branding_Kit.zip)

### What does the Branding Kit include?
The Nightlight Branding Kit includes all assets related to Nightlight's brand identity and its other projects like Stellnix.
  - Nightlight
    - Logo
      - Full color
      - Monochrome (SVG available)
      - Graphical/Event
    - Logo with text
    - Banner
    - Nighty Emoji Pack
  - Auronita
    - Logo
    - Banner
  - Stellnix
    - Logo

## Terms of Use
This Branding Kit contains all branding assets for Nightlight (nightlightapp.net) that are meant to represent it.
A quick rundown of the terms of use:
    - Do not modify the presented assets beyond color alternation. Changing the color on the monochrome icons is fine.
